Zoom in — Instagram

Four formats, four rule sets, one grid

The feed is not a stream of posts. It is a rotation, and each slot has a format with its own skill file and its own staging rules. Photography and product frames come out of moodcase itself: the agents hold their own seat on the product — Leonie, an invented colleague whose account exists so the workflow has real credentials — and reach into her library through the moodcase MCP, pulling what they need by CDN link. The marketing runs on the product it describes.

  • AP → PX
    The same asset, twice

    Every product frame runs on a blurred, darkened copy of the photograph sitting next to it. The pair is deliberate: you see the image, then you see it inside the product.

  • VA · MH
    Midnight punctuates

    Quote cards and hack cards interrupt the photography at a fixed interval. Without them the grid reads as a gallery instead of a product feed.

  • Rotation
    The block is the unit

    Posts are planned nine to twelve at a time and judged as a shape, never one at a time. What you are looking at is one turn of a 21-week rotation.

What's next

Three things not built yet

The system publishes reliably. What it does not yet do is choose its own pictures for an article, or plan the blog and the social surfaces as one thing.

In progress

Article imagery, automated

Choosing and placing hero and inline images is the one manual step left in the blog run. The moodcase MCP already serves the Instagram grid — the same route will select article imagery from the library, so a finished article arrives with its pictures already in place.

Next

One plan, not two

The blog calendar and the social calendar are planned separately today. Merging them means an article and its LinkedIn and Instagram derivatives are scheduled from a single decision, instead of being reconciled afterwards.

Next

Amplification by default

Every published article should generate its full run across both LinkedIn accounts and the grid, timed to publication rather than picked up later. Consistency stops being a review job and becomes a property of the plan.

The system is the asset. Content conforms to it — never the other way around. From the moodcase system files.
